Privacy Notice

Event: THE FIRST CHAPTER – 2026 (Freshers' Welcome)

Institution: Ethos Senior Secondary School, Barpeta

1. What data do we collect?

During registration, we collect your Full Name, Roll Number, Academic Interests, Personality Responses, Dreams, Expectations, and your Message to your Future Self.

2. How will we use your data?

Your data is used solely for event registration, generating your personalized invitation and ticket, and creating a fun, memorable experience for the Freshers' Welcome. Your responses may be used in slideshows or event materials (only if you consent during the questionnaire).

3. How is your data protected?

All data is securely stored in Google Cloud Firestore. Your private questionnaire responses are only accessible to authorized administrators of Ethos Senior Secondary School. Your QR code contains only a random ticket ID, not your personal information.

4. Data Retention

Your data will be kept securely for the duration of the event and for archival purposes. If you wish to have your data removed after the event, please contact the school administration.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about your data, please reach out to the event organizers.
